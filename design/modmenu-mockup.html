Layout Rules

  • Client home stays clean. Cheats appear in mod menu during game session flow (3A).
  • Left/main: header + status + scrollable content. Right/side: app icon, tabs, close.
  • No long messy sections; grouped by tabs.
  • Temp status/toast and persistent notification states are visible.
  • Finding shot shows a rotating spinner/ball indicator.

Feature List

  • Status/Test: session status, quick test, launch 8BP, stop, notification state.
  • Lines/Aim: Show lines, Keep lines after shot, Power lock, Default power, Show pockets, Spin, Line opacity, Prediction test.
  • Auto Play: Enable auto play, mode, Failed break, finding-shot indicator, autoplay notifications.
  • 8 Ball: Prioritize 9th ball, Paralysis opponent turn, Clean up shot, Golden shot target.
  • Settings: notifications, test behavior, clean client options; no auto-queue UI.
